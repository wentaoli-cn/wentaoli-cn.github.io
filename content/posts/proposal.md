Although she said yes, I still feel that I didn't do as well as I had hoped. I was a little too nervous, and the moment didn't turn out as perfectly as I had imagined.

Still, it became one of the most meaningful days of my life. I hope that when our wedding day comes, I won't make the same mistake again, and that I'll be able to stay calm and fully enjoy every moment with her.
